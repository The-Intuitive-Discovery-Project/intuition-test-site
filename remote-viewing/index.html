Remote Viewing 101

Learn the method, settle yourself before each session, work on paper, then reveal a real photo target only after you finish.

What remote viewing is

Remote viewing is a structured attempt to describe a target that is hidden from you through normal sensory means. You are not trying to name the target as quickly as possible. You are trying to notice simple information first: shape, temperature, color, texture, movement, size, sound, atmosphere, and spatial relationships.

My way, not the only way: this course gives you a structure to start with. Once you understand the process, use what actually helps you. You do not have to copy somebody else's exact ritual forever.

Before every session

1. Get physically comfortable

Sit somewhere you can write and sketch. Reduce interruptions. You want quiet and alert, not half asleep.

2. Settle for at least five minutes

Breathing, grounding, stillness, or soft focus are all fine. The practice tab deliberately keeps the target ID hidden for the first five minutes.

3. Drop the pressure

You do not have to identify the target. Notice what comes first. A few simple impressions can be more useful than a dramatic guess.

4. Work on paper

The website gives the target and feedback. Your actual session belongs on the printable worksheet so you can write naturally and make quick sketches.

Raw impression vs. AOL

Raw impression: tall, metallic, open underneath, repeating vertical lines, cool, outdoor.

AOL (Analytical Overlay): “bridge.”

If your brain suddenly names the target, write the guess in the AOL area of the worksheet, then go back to describing. The guess can be wrong while several raw impressions are still useful.

Your basic session flow

Settle

Press Begin Session and spend at least five quiet minutes before the target number becomes available.

Receive the blind ID

You will get a meaningless number such as RV-4827-1936. It tells you nothing about the target.

View on paper

Use the worksheet: first impression, gestalt, sensory qualities, dimensions, movement, two quick sketches, AOL, and a raw summary.

Reveal and compare

Only when your paper session is finished do you reveal the target photo. Keep misses. Do not stretch vague notes into hits.

How much should you practice?

I am not going to tell you that you are only “allowed” to do a certain number. Practice how you want. After your third completed session in a day, the app will simply remind you that taking a break might help you come back fresh. You can ignore that and keep going.

Remote viewing is experimental and has a disputed scientific history. Treat this as structured intuition practice, not a reliable way to make medical, legal, financial, safety, or other high-stakes decisions.

How the finished version will store targets

Cloudflare R2

Stores the real target photographs and the printable worksheet file. Target images are not exposed in the public page before reveal.

D1 database

Stores target IDs, which R2 object belongs to each target, whether a target has been used, and small session metadata if we decide we need it.

Worker API

Acts as the locked door. Before the session is finished it returns only the blind target ID. After reveal, it returns the authorized image.

What we do not need to store: your handwritten worksheet, sketches, impressions, or AOL notes. Those can stay completely offline and private.
