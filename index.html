Sound Intuition

Before hearing a sound, take a quiet moment and notice whatever comes to you. You might receive a feeling, word, image, place, color, emotion, temperature, object, animal, or something that doesn't make sense yet.

Example only: You might write things like “cold,” “outside,” “something moving,” “blue,” or “uneasy.” These are only examples of the kinds of impressions you may notice — they are not clues about the upcoming sound.

Don't try to figure it out. Write your impressions first. Once you lock them in, you'll hear the target and can compare what you received with what was actually there.
