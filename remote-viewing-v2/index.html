Remote Viewing 101

Learn a structured paper-first method, receive a blind target ID, and compare your finished session with a real physical site. V2 now uses one preselected primary feedback photo per active target instead of sending you to an image-search page.

What remote viewing means in this course

You begin blind. The target is a real physical site, but you receive only a meaningless ID such as RV-6047-3524. Record raw information first: sensation, shape, line, motion, texture, temperature, sound, scale, atmosphere, and spatial relationships.

The target is the site, not the photograph. The selected photo is controlled feedback after the session. It is one view of the location, not the thing you were supposed to reproduce.

Why this is different from the other blind-reveal exercises

The difference is the method: a blind reference ID, a paper transcript, structured raw-data collection, sketches, AOL discipline, a clear END SESSION point, and fixed feedback about a real site.

Before every session

1. Get comfortable

Sit somewhere you can write and sketch without being interrupted. Relax, but stay alert.

2. Take at least five quiet minutes

This is our beginner routine, not a universal historical rule. Breathe, ground, sit in stillness, or use soft focus if it helps.

3. Do not force a picture

You may get words, body sensations, sounds, lines, movement, texture, or a brief image. Let information arrive however it arrives.

4. Work on paper

The app gives the blind ID and later the feedback. Your actual viewing session stays on the printable worksheet.

Beginner progression

First contact

Write the target ID and notice what happens immediately. The optional ideogram is one quick spontaneous pen mark, not a drawing of the target.

Sensory data

Record raw qualities such as color/light, texture, temperature, sound, smell/taste, atmosphere, or body sense.

Shape and space

Notice lines, movement, scale, separation, enclosure, direction, and relationships. Sketch quickly.

AOL: when your mind names the target

Raw data: tall, hard, metallic, open underneath, repeating lines, outdoor.

AOL / interpretation: bridge.

Write the interpretation in the AOL area, then return to description. A label can be wrong while several raw impressions are useful.

End first. Reveal second.

When you feel finished, write END SESSION and the end time. Do not change the session after that. Then reveal the target and compare it with your unedited notes.

Do not rescue a weak session by stretching vague words into matches. Clear misses stay misses. The point is to learn your own signal.

Feedback discipline

V2 now shows a single preselected primary photo immediately after reveal. That photo, its attribution, and the original remote-viewing source are fixed before your session. This reduces the temptation to browse many images until one seems to fit your notes.

Remote viewing has a documented experimental and government-research history, but its scientific interpretation and reliability remain disputed. Use this as structured intuition practice, not as a substitute for evidence or for medical, legal, financial, safety, or other high-stakes decisions.
